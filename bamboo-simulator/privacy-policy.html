Bamboo Simulator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Bamboo Simulator ("the app", Android package com.cya.bamboosimulator) handles. The app is developed by CYA ("we").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. There is no sign-in.

Your game progress (gold, upgrades, best height, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one, for example to continue a round or double your gold.

To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app asks for it through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings on the main menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising SDK described above, the app does not send your data to third parties.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change (for example, if crash reporting or analytics are added), and change the "Last updated" date above.
